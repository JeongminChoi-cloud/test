The Runway Palette Archive

About

The Runway Palette Archive is an index-based project focused on runway imagery and color relationships.

This project began from a deeply personal experience. I studied fashion design and previously worked as a designer at WE11DONE in Korea. When searching for references, I often found it difficult to describe garments through language alone. In many cases, I could not recall the exact season, year, or even the brand of a collection. What remained most vividly in my memory was not the name or structure of the garments, but the overall mood of the collection—its color.

This project originates from that limitation. Instead of relying on traditional systems such as designer, season, or brand, The Runway Palette Archive proposes an alternative way of organizing fashion imagery through color. Color becomes the primary index, functioning as both a visual and emotional entry point into the archive.

The interface is built around this idea. A central color node and an interactive slider allow users to navigate across a spectrum of tones. As the color shifts, corresponding runway images gradually appear, forming a network of visual connections. This system reflects how memory operates—fluid, associative, and often anchored in sensation rather than factual information.

By prioritizing color as a navigational structure, the archive redefines how fashion collections can be accessed and remembered. It transforms subjective memory into a functional interface, offering a more intuitive and sensory-driven way of exploring runway imagery.
